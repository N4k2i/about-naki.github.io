## Overview

Noctaruks are a distinct species and are not sawians.

## Established visual traits

The current Markov record establishes several Noctaruk-associated traits:

- Black-and-white fur
- Humanoid shark-like body structure
- Digitigrade legs
- Shark tail
- Fin structures
- Defined snout

## Canon distinction

The species should be treated as separate from sawians unless a future canon update explicitly changes that rule.
