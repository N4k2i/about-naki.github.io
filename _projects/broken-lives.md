A long-form writing project where progression mechanics are part of the story rather than background decoration.

## Core systems

The project is built to support systems such as:

- Levels
- Statistics
- Classes and roles
- Skills and abilities
- Quests and objectives
- Experience gain
- System notifications
- Unlocks
- Evolutions
- Territory progression
- Character-specific growth

## Why the system matters

I like LitRPG systems most when they change how characters make decisions. Numbers and unlocks should create consequences, tradeoffs, and new possibilities instead of existing only as decoration.

## Worldbuilding direction

The progression structure is tied to Expie bodies, environments, territory, alliances, antagonists, and the larger cave progression. The intent is for advancement to feel like part of the world's logic.
