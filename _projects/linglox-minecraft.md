A Minecraft project inspired by the visual language, puzzle structure, colors, materials, and systems associated with Lingo and Linglox.

## Planned content areas

- Color systems
- Materials
- Checkers
- Chains
- Prompt-inspired mechanics
- Puzzle structures
- Advancement systems
- Progression
- Custom items and blocks
- Themed environments

## Scale

One of the defining goals is breadth. I like the idea of a mod where there are always more categories, goals, and advancement paths to discover.

The advancement structure is intentionally ambitious and can serve as both a guide and a long-term completion system.
