TSI Math Prep started as an educational Minecraft Java mod concept and expanded toward a broader game-like learning experience.

## Main idea

Instead of presenting practice as a worksheet inside Minecraft, the project aims to turn learning into progression.

Potential systems include:

- Lessons
- Question banks
- Difficulty tiers
- Mastery tracking
- Interactive challenges
- Optional reading content
- Optional writing content
- Optional science content
- Achievements
- Unlocks
- Rewards
- Difficulty scaling

## Design goal

The educational part should remain real, but the surrounding structure should give players reasons to continue beyond simply answering another question.

That makes feedback, progression pacing, difficulty, and reward design just as important as the questions themselves.
