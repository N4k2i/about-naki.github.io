This is one of my major long-form writing and worldbuilding projects. It is designed around character relationships, continuity, original species, setting development, and a large internal lore structure.

## What I enjoy building in it

- Character relationships and long-running arcs
- Original species and nonhuman character designs
- Locations and setting history
- Rules that keep the world internally consistent
- Character-specific visual motifs
- Connections between personal story beats and larger world events
- A growing archive of canon decisions

## Project philosophy

The goal is not just to have a list of characters and events. I like building enough context that the world feels like it exists beyond whichever scene is currently being written.

That means tracking what characters know, what they do not know, where they were during major events, how relationships changed, what is canon, and which older ideas have been replaced.

## Status

The project is actively evolving, so this page is intentionally structured like a living record rather than a final encyclopedia entry.
