The site started as a single long About Me page.

This build moves much more of the content into reusable Jekyll structures:

- Collections for projects
- A separate writing archive
- Dedicated profile pages
- A command palette
- Shared layouts and includes
- Data-driven navigation
- A larger design system
- More client-side accessibility and preference controls

The goal is to make future expansion easier instead of turning `index.html` into an impossible wall of markup.
