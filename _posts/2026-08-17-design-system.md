The visual direction is intentionally somewhere between a personal site, terminal, database, and game UI.

The design system uses:

- Very dark backgrounds
- Purple as the primary accent
- Pink and blue as secondary accents
- Monospace labels
- Large sans-serif headings
- Glass-like cards
- Thin glowing borders
- Subtle noise and star layers
- Reduced-motion support

The goal is dense information without sacrificing readability.
