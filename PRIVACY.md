# Public-site privacy checklist

Before publishing:

- Do not add your street address.
- Do not publish your phone number.
- Avoid publishing private email addresses unless you intentionally want them public.
- Do not expose IP addresses, account-recovery evidence, passwords, tokens, or security logs.
- Be cautious with exact routines and schedules.
- Review every external link before publishing.
- Remove location metadata from personal photos if appropriate.
