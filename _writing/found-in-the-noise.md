## Overview

This writing project is built around a large continuity that can be revised as the story develops. I treat canon decisions as part of the writing process rather than assuming every early idea has to remain forever.

## What gets tracked

- Current canon cast
- Removed or superseded ideas
- Character relationships
- Family relationships
- Species details
- Visual design rules
- Timeline placement
- Important setting rules
- Chapter-level continuity
- Author notes

A dedicated writing page lets the main project archive stay concise while still providing a place for deeper story-specific information.
