The cache named the record `james` before the person crossed.

**Dossier 03. James.**  
MAKO CLOSE CIRCLE. he/him. open.  
Story role: one of Mako's closest friends; shorthand familiarity.

Jonathan hated how quickly he recognized the fields. The bible had trained him to retrieve them as
facts, and now the facts were attached to a breathing person occupying space in his family's home.
The description did not become false when the person became real. It became insufficient.

James arrived looking for Mako before he looked for himself. Relief lasted less than a
second before the house demanded explanation. Their old shorthand survived the crossing, proof that
whole histories existed between scenes Jonathan had never written.

The traits in the bible—reliable , familiar , witty , loyal , practical , observant , steady , casual , protective , adaptable—did not arrive as a checklist. They arrived as competing instincts
under pressure. That difference became one of the first rules of the house:

**A dossier may explain. It does not get to order.**

Jonathan eventually showed James the line labeled **WANTS**:

> keep old friendship easy without taking it for granted

Then the line labeled **NEEDS**:

> say when something has changed instead of relying only on history

There was no correct reaction. Some laughed. Some went quiet. Some argued with the wording.
The most unsettling response was recognition.

By dawn, the house contained twenty-five people who remembered being fictional and six real-world
witnesses who could no longer agree on what the word *fictional* was supposed to mean.

Shadow and Scout continued making their own determinations.
