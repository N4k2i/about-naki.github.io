The cache named the record `ivan` before the person crossed.

**Dossier 06. Ivan.**  
ANTAGONIST / INCIDENT. he/him. open.  
Story role: source of the gun incident and lasting safety rupture.

Jonathan hated how quickly he recognized the fields. The bible had trained him to retrieve them as
facts, and now the facts were attached to a breathing person occupying space in his family's home.
The description did not become false when the person became real. It became insufficient.

Ivan's arrival changed the rules instantly. Nobody forgot what the bible called him:
the source of the gun incident and the lasting safety rupture. Mako positioned himself between Ivan
and the household. Jonathan faced a contradiction: treating the dossier as a verdict felt monstrous;
ignoring it felt irresponsible.

The traits in the bible—volatile , defensive , reckless , controlling , unpredictable , resentful , argumentative , strategic when cornered ,—did not arrive as a checklist. They arrived as competing instincts
under pressure. That difference became one of the first rules of the house:

**A dossier may explain. It does not get to order.**

Jonathan eventually showed Ivan the line labeled **WANTS**:

> avoid consequences and retain control of the story around his actions

Then the line labeled **NEEDS**:

> face accountability rather than treating explanation as absolution

There was no correct reaction. Some laughed. Some went quiet. Some argued with the wording.
The most unsettling response was recognition.

By dawn, the house contained twenty-five people who remembered being fictional and six real-world
witnesses who could no longer agree on what the word *fictional* was supposed to mean.

Shadow and Scout continued making their own determinations.
