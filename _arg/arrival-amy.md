The cache named the record `amy` before the person crossed.

**Dossier 08. Amy.**  
MAKO HOUSEHOLD. she/her. human-presenting; exact type open.  
Story role: Mako's biological little sister; domestic grounding.

Jonathan hated how quickly he recognized the fields. The bible had trained him to retrieve them as
facts, and now the facts were attached to a breathing person occupying space in his family's home.
The description did not become false when the person became real. It became insufficient.

The first person to say "Amy" caused two heads to turn. Amy M. looked at the fictional
Amy; the fictional Amy looked back. The joke worked until Amy asked whether "from the story" was
going to be attached to her name forever.

The traits in the bible—bright , observant , independent , affectionate , curious , practical , stubborn , expressive , perceptive , playful—did not arrive as a checklist. They arrived as competing instincts
under pressure. That difference became one of the first rules of the house:

**A dossier may explain. It does not get to order.**

Jonathan eventually showed Amy the line labeled **WANTS**:

> have room to grow without everyone pre-solving her life

Then the line labeled **NEEDS**:

> be allowed to make age-appropriate mistakes

There was no correct reaction. Some laughed. Some went quiet. Some argued with the wording.
The most unsettling response was recognition.

By dawn, the house contained twenty-four people who remembered being fictional and six real-world
witnesses who could no longer agree on what the word *fictional* was supposed to mean.

Shadow and Scout continued making their own determinations.
