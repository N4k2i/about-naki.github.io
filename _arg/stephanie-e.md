Stephanie E. did not solve the metaphysics. She solved the room.

Twenty-five unexpected people needed water, places to sit, places to sleep, privacy, and rules.
Some had anatomy ordinary furniture had never been designed around. Some needed quiet. Some needed
distance from Ivan. Nobody had prepared the household because no household could be prepared.

She began asking **names instead of roles**.

Not "Which one is the lawyer?"—Lime.  
Not "Which one is the shark?"—Markov.  
Not "Which one is Naki's boyfriend?"—Blade.  
Not "Which one is the antagonist?"—Emily.

The characters noticed.

Later, when the house was finally quiet, Stephanie asked Jonathan whether he had ever written a scene
where all twenty-five got to be safe at the same time.

Jonathan tried to remember.

The delay before his answer was the answer.
