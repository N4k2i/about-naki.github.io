Shadow distrusted the arrivals in a way no character bible could interpret.

The cat had no interest in canon, authorship, narrative role, species classification, or legal
existence. Shadow cared about smell, sound, movement, territory, and whether twenty-five bodies had
disrupted the house.

Markov received the longest inspection from a safe distance. Lime's wings received no approval.
Raine's propeller hat was watched with offended concentration. Ivan was avoided.

This became important later.

When the first memory failures began, humans sometimes forgot who occupied a chair.

Shadow did not.

Shadow continued staring at the apparently empty seat.

`WITNESS_CLASS: FELINE`
