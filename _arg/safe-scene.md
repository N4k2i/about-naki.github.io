Jonathan made a private document.

Not canon. Not a chapter.

Just a scene where everybody had space, money, safety, and ordinary time.

Naki found it because the archive surfaced it.

The scene was kind.

That made it worse.

Jonathan had written Mako relaxing, Markov secure, Amy laughing, Fear understood, Blade and Naki
uncomplicated, Ivan contained, Emily conceding something morally important.

Naki asked why Jonathan had written their reactions instead of writing the room and letting them
enter it themselves.

Jonathan deleted the scene.

The next day he began designing an actual room instead.

Fiction had trained him to solve people.

Reality required him to make space.
