Shadow preferred quiet people. Scout preferred interesting movement.

Their habits built a map of the house.

Shadow sometimes slept near Doc. Scout followed Naki. Both were cautious around Ivan. Markov learned
how to move his tail without triggering a chase response. Lime learned a wingtip under a blanket
could become a cat problem. Zucchi accumulated drawings of paws stepping onto paper.

The cats made the cast part of routine.

That should have been comforting.

Jonathan began fearing the day the routine would break.

If the characters vanished, humans could argue about memory.

Shadow and Scout would still wait at doors.
