Naki vanished from the shoulders upward for less than a second.

His glasses fell.

That broke everybody.

A clean digital glitch might still have felt like an anomaly.

Instead, the glasses hit the floor.

Blade moved before Jonathan.

Naki returned on his knees, one hand searching for glasses he could not see.

Jonathan picked them up and froze.

Weight. Smudges. Warmth from Naki's face.

An object belonging to a person the universe had just failed to render.

Naki took them back.

"Don't put this in the story."

Jonathan said okay.

The ARG includes the event anyway because the site wrote the record itself.

That is when Jonathan stopped trusting the ARG as his creation.
