**Dossier 02. Mako.**  
CORE CAST / HOUSEHOLD. he/him. black-furred horned hybrid; exact species open.  
Story role: social gravity; controlled analyst; household anchor.

Jonathan hated how quickly he recognized the fields. The bible had trained him to retrieve them as
facts, and now the facts were attached to a breathing person occupying space in his family's home.
The description did not become false when the person became real. It became insufficient.

Mako's first act was inventory. He counted doors, exits, people, animals, and then the
members of his household. When Markov and Amy had not appeared yet, his restraint became frighteningly
still. "Where are they?" Jonathan had no answer that was not an admission of powerlessness.

The traits in the bible—observant , restrained , analytical , artistically attentive , protective , selective , patient , private , dryly amused ,—did not arrive as a checklist. They arrived as competing instincts
under pressure. That difference became one of the first rules of the house:

**A dossier may explain. It does not get to order.**

Jonathan eventually showed Mako the line labeled **WANTS**:

> keep people safe while preserving a functional home and friend network

Then the line labeled **NEEDS**:

> trust others with autonomy instead of solving everything himself

There was no correct reaction. Some laughed. Some went quiet. Some argued with the wording.
The most unsettling response was recognition.

By dawn, the house contained twenty-five people who remembered being fictional and six real-world
witnesses who could no longer agree on what the word *fictional* was supposed to mean.

Shadow and Scout continued making their own determinations.
