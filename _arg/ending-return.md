The entire cast returned immediately.

The story stabilized.

The household remembered.

For a while.

Then details softened.

Stephanie E. remembered guests but not every name.

Jason W. remembered the protocol but not what required it.

Amy M. remembered another Amy and could no longer picture her face.

Shadow and Scout waited at doors less often.

Jonathan retained the most because he kept rereading the bible.

That became its own horror.

He could recite Naki's eye colors and no longer remember the sound of Naki saying,
"Don't list me."

A safe story without a return path was still a one-way door.
