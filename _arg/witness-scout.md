The signal rises to 0.0002%.

Scout runs to the hallway.

Jonathan follows.

The doorway opens only wide enough for a hand.

Black-and-white fur.

Clawed fingers.

Markov.

Scout reaches him this time.

One brief sniff.

One cautious head bump against the hand.

Then the signal falls.

The hand disappears.

Scout waits three seconds and walks away.

Jonathan does not cry until later.

The archive records only:

`CONTACT SUCCESSFUL.`

`DURATION: 00:00:04.`

`WITNESS: SCOUT.`

`VISITOR: MARKOV.`

No metaphor is required.
