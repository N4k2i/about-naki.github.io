Rain arrived without symbolism.

Nobody confessed. Nobody died. No chapter reached an emotional turn.

It simply rained.

Zucchi watched water race down the glass and said she expected Jonathan to describe it more
dramatically.

Jonathan said real weather did not care about scene tone.

Redsky asked whether that made it freer.

Naki said maybe lonelier.

Markov stood by the door to smell the air.

Mako stayed close enough to stop himself from telling Markov not to.

The bible said family closeness should not erase autonomy.

For once the sentence helped instead of hurt.
