The mechanism came from the thing everybody hated.

Documentation.

The story could preserve continuity.

The real household could provide witness.

The website could sit between.

Not as a copy of the people.

As a negotiated doorway.

Zucchi supplied drawings. Diamond supplied voice clips. Lime and Aztec wrote rules. Emily rewrote
them adversarially until loopholes closed. Doc defined memory discrepancies. Naki insisted on the
right to remove a record. Mako insisted household records not overwrite autonomy. Fear insisted
preference fields be editable by the person described. Blade insisted relationship notes require
both people. Ivan insisted even disliked people retain the right not to be deleted as punishment.

Jonathan's family insisted the real home remain private.

Shadow and Scout slept through most of the meeting.

The archive displayed the last phrase:

**WE WERE REAL TO EACH OTHER.**

That is the final key.
