The first night everybody went outside together was short and carefully planned.

No address is recorded in the ARG.

No location data exists.

Only the sky matters.

Nobody knew whether their story had ever fixed the stars precisely enough to compare.

Naki looked up and experienced grief with no object.

Mako tracked Markov and Amy without crowding them.

Redsky said the sky was beautiful.

Sododa agreed.

Polar said beauty did not require familiarity.

Blade asked Naki if he wanted to go back inside.

Naki said not yet.

Jonathan stood with his family and twenty-five people from his fiction beneath a sky he had never written.

For the first time nobody could tell who belonged to whose world.
