By sunrise, metaphysics had become furniture.

Blankets covered floors. Chairs moved. Lime had to account for wings. Markov had to account for a
tail. Isaac had to find a posture that did not punish digitigrade legs. Everybody discovered that
"sleep wherever" was not a plan when fear made ordinary proximity feel loaded.

Mako kept Markov and Amy within sight.

Naki and Blade stayed near one another without making the relationship perform.

Fear chose a quieter place and made the choice herself.

Polar thanked Stephanie E. with such formal sincerity that she had to look away.

Ivan remained separated by agreement from the people who most needed distance.

Jonathan did not sleep.

Around 06:40, Scout walked across the room, stepping around bodies that should not have existed, and
curled up beside a person whose name had been invented years earlier.

The house looked less like a miracle than an evacuation center.
