Blade stared at the line:

**friendship → deliberately casual affection → boyfriend**

An arrow could summarize it.

An arrow could not contain the nights between categories.

Blade asked Jonathan when the second stage became the third.

Jonathan tried to remember the chapter decision.

Blade stopped him.

"Not for you. For us."

Jonathan had no answer.

Naki sat beside the desk. Blade sat beside him.

Neither asked Jonathan to define them again.
