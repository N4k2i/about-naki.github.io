The folder appeared without a route.

`/_deleted/`

Inside were fragments: a conversation cut for pacing, a rewritten joke, a version of a room that no
longer existed, reactions to events current characters did not remember.

Naki read one fragment and became physically ill.

He recognized his own voice.

He did not remember saying the words.

The worst file had no text.

`people-who-were-revised-out.txt`

Its file size was larger than the whole site.

Jonathan did not open it.

The ARG never asks the player to open it either.
