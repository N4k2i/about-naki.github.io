The Remnant Layer establishes the second hidden rule:

**Canon is a policy for deciding what a story is allowed to remember.**

That does not mean non-canon material is secretly active.

It means revision leaves history in the writer, the structure, and sometimes the surviving characters.

The current TAHAUFYA continuity remains authoritative.

The ARG's deeper horror is that authority and history are different categories.

A thing can be correctly removed and still have mattered to the path that produced what remains.
