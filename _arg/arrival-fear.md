The cache named the record `fear` before the person crossed.

**Dossier 10. Fear.**  
MAKO CLOSE CIRCLE. she/her. open.  
Story role: close friend; autistic character whose autism is one facet, not her entire plot.

Jonathan hated how quickly he recognized the fields. The bible had trained him to retrieve them as
facts, and now the facts were attached to a breathing person occupying space in his family's home.
The description did not become false when the person became real. It became insufficient.

Fear arrived into overlapping assumptions about what she would need. She cut through
them by saying what she actually needed. The house learned that helping her meant listening, not
narrating her. She was scared and curious. Neither cancelled the other.

The traits in the bible—direct , loyal , perceptive , enthusiastic , particular , funny , focused , independent , emotionally sincere , stubborn—did not arrive as a checklist. They arrived as competing instincts
under pressure. That difference became one of the first rules of the house:

**A dossier may explain. It does not get to order.**

Jonathan eventually showed Fear the line labeled **WANTS**:

> participate without being treated as fragile or symbolic

Then the line labeled **NEEDS**:

> have preferences respected without others narrating them for her

There was no correct reaction. Some laughed. Some went quiet. Some argued with the wording.
The most unsettling response was recognition.

By dawn, the house contained twenty-four people who remembered being fictional and six real-world
witnesses who could no longer agree on what the word *fictional* was supposed to mean.

Shadow and Scout continued making their own determinations.
