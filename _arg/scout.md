Scout was bolder.

Where Shadow withdrew and observed, Scout investigated. He sniffed unfamiliar shoes, fur, tails,
and the edge of Lime's folded wing. He followed Naki down the hallway. He sat outside the room where
Blade and Naki talked after everybody stopped asking them questions.

Scout learned routines faster than the humans did.

Morning meant movement in the kitchen. Markov sitting on the floor meant investigation was allowed.
Zucchi drawing meant paper might move. Joel moving too quickly meant caution.

The characters learned Scout's routines in return.

That mutual recognition became one of the most ordinary things in the ARG and later one of the most
painful.

A cat either expects somebody to return to the same chair tomorrow or does not.
