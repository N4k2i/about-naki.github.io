Jonathan learns the hardest archival lesson:

A removed character should remain removed when privacy, continuity, or authorial choice requires it.

But pretending the old version never existed can damage understanding of how the current version formed.

The ARG therefore stores **structural remnants without active profiles**.

Examples:

- "an earlier social cluster existed here"
- "this relationship slot was once occupied"
- "this scene was rewritten around a cast reduction"
- "this emotional beat survived even though its original trigger did not"

No removed character is reintroduced.

The lore is about revision itself.

At the bottom:

`DELETED ≠ DEAD`

What phrase does that imply when punctuation is removed?
