The line was explicit:

**The gun incident remains serious and consequential. Injury, fear, trust rupture, and legal aftermath
are not reset for comedy.**

Ivan called it biased.

Lime said a record of harm was not bias merely because the person responsible disliked it.

Emily objected to some phrasing from a defense perspective.

Aztec separated whether the incident happened from how it should be interpreted.

Jonathan sat at the edge of the room thinking about the fact that he had written every position.

Ivan looked at him.

"You made the argument unwinnable."

Jonathan said no.

"Then let me win it."

Jonathan could not.

The silence was the closest thing the room had produced to a verdict.
