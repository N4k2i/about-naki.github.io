The log said:

`AMY IS IN THE KITCHEN.`

Amy M. was there alone.

The fictional Amy was in the hallway.

The archive had lost the distinction again.

Amy M. took the index card with **AMY** written on it and handed it to her.

"Keep it."

Amy asked what Amy M. would use.

"I've already got mine."

"What if it forgets the M. too?"

"Then we write it again."

Jonathan hated the record because it sounded hopeful.

Hope made failure sharper.
