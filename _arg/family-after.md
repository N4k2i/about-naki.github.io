Stephanie E. kept one handwritten roster.

Jason W. kept the forgetting protocol.

Amy M. kept a copy of the failed photograph.

Jonathan kept everything else and spent weeks deciding how much should remain public.

The rule held:

**Family conversations are not automatically content.**

Shadow and Scout returned to ordinary routines slowly.

Sometimes one cat stared down the hallway.

Sometimes both did.

Nobody treated it as proof.

Nobody treated it as nothing.

The house became normal again in the practical sense.

Normal was no longer an innocent word.
