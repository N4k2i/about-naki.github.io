Jason W. designed it after the first failures.

Every room with characters had a written roster.

If somebody seemed absent:

1. Check where Shadow and Scout are looking.
2. Check physical effects—chair compression, moving objects, sound, warmth.
3. Read the person's chosen sentence.
4. Say the person's name.
5. Do not improvise memories.

The last rule mattered.

When the mind repaired a gap, it invented plausible explanations:

`Nobody was sitting there.`  
`That blanket was always folded that way.`  
`There were only twenty-four arrivals.`

The protocol treated certainty as a symptom.

Aztec approved.

Emily did too.
