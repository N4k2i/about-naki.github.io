Jason W. approached the situation like a problem that had arrived without instructions.

Count people. Count rooms. Identify hazards. Establish which doors needed to remain open, who
required separate space, what technology worked, and which assumptions existed only because everybody
was frightened.

Lime and Aztec appreciated the distinction between fact and inference. Doc appreciated somebody else
writing observations down. Isaac helped with physical tasks until Jason told him he did not have to
become the default worker just because he was good at it.

Jason eventually looked at Jonathan's screen.

"If they're here, what happens to the story?"

Jonathan opened the website.

Every dossier still existed.

The story had not lost them.

It had copied them.

Nobody knew whether that was better.
