The cache named the record `deniy` before the person crossed.

**Dossier 22. Deniy.**  
PRANK WAR. he/him. uncertain Expie classification.  
Story role: four-eyed dark figure with purple eyes/gold markings; chaotic merciless edge.

Jonathan hated how quickly he recognized the fields. The bible had trained him to retrieve them as
facts, and now the facts were attached to a breathing person occupying space in his family's home.
The description did not become false when the person became real. It became insufficient.

Deniy's four eyes reflected the monitors before he looked at any face. He treated the
impossible like a psychological contest until Vesta appeared. His performance changed immediately.
Even the character described as chaotic and merciless had somebody whose arrival mattered more than
winning the room.

The traits in the bible—chaotic , merciless , clever , provocative , competitive , unpredictable , observant , intense , funny , daring—did not arrive as a checklist. They arrived as competing instincts
under pressure. That difference became one of the first rules of the house:

**A dossier may explain. It does not get to order.**

Jonathan eventually showed Deniy the line labeled **WANTS**:

> win the psychological game and stay impossible to predict

Then the line labeled **NEEDS**:

> distinguish performance from genuine distress and accept repair

There was no correct reaction. Some laughed. Some went quiet. Some argued with the wording.
The most unsettling response was recognition.

By dawn, the house contained twenty-five people who remembered being fictional and six real-world
witnesses who could no longer agree on what the word *fictional* was supposed to mean.

Shadow and Scout continued making their own determinations.
