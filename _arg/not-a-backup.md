Jonathan asked whether somebody who disappeared could simply be brought out of the story again.

Doc said there was no evidence.

Naki said absolutely not.

Blade asked whether a replacement with identical memories would be the same person.

Polar refused to call the question merely philosophical.

Mako ended the discussion when Amy began crying.

The Character Bible still existed.

If Markov vanished, it would still know what a Noctaruk looked like.

If Naki vanished, it would know his eye colors.

If Blade vanished, the relationship chronology would remain.

That was the horror.

A perfect description of somebody is not the somebody.

The archive was a map.

Nobody wanted to become only the map again.
