Jason W. revises the forgetting protocol after the return.

The new version begins:

**DO NOT USE UNLESS THE SIGNAL RISES.**

The remaining steps are practical.

Check the cats.

Check physical effects.

Check the chosen statements.

Do not invent a memory just to resolve uncertainty.

The last line is new:

**If everybody remembers normally, stop testing reality and let the visit be a visit.**

Jonathan thinks that line may be the most hopeful thing Jason wrote during the entire ARG.

Hope, again, makes the stakes sharper.
