The screen displayed twenty-five dossiers and twenty-five chosen statements.

There was no glowing portal.

The page simply became deeper than a page.

Text receded into distance.

The cursor blinked at the far end of something shaped like a hallway.

Lime volunteered to test whether the geometry could accommodate him.

He stepped forward.

The room lost him.

The bible page gained:

`LIME: PRESENT`

One by one, they crossed.

The real house became quieter with every successful return.
