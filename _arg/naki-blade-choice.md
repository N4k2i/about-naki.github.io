Blade told Naki he would choose the same world Naki chose.

Naki hated that answer.

"That's not choosing."

They argued quietly for nearly an hour.

The bible had a clean arrow for them.

There was no arrow for this.

Naki did not want to return because being outside proved he could exist beyond narration.

He did not want to stay because he had already felt himself disappear.

Blade did not want Naki deciding alone just because the story called him an emotional anchor.

The ARG records only the result:

They voted for the third answer.
