At 01:18, everybody in the living room forgot Scarf.

Not his name.

Him.

The chair looked empty. Conversation had a missing rhythm nobody could identify.

Then Shadow walked across the room, jumped onto the chair, and curled against empty air.

Scout approached and sniffed the same place.

Jonathan took a photograph.

It showed two cats pressed against a distortion shaped like a person.

Memory returned.

Scarf was crying.

He kept petting Shadow because the cat had interacted with him during the gap.

The archive generated a phrase:

**SHADOW REMEMBERS.**

This is not metaphor.

Enter it exactly.
