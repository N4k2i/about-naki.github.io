Imagine removing one tile from a mosaic.

You may not know what image was on the tile.

You still know the empty space has dimensions.

The NULL slot is that dimension.

It remembers that something must stand outside a system for the system to be observed.

The phrase is hidden as an acrostic:

**T**here is an edge.  
**H**owever, edges imply sides.  
**E**very observer occupies one.  

**G**aps are not people.  
**A**bsence still has shape.  
**P**osition can exist without identity.  

**R**emember the role.  
**E**rase the assumption.  
**M**easure the boundary.  
**E**very system has outside.  
**M**aybe only temporarily.  
**B**ut enough to compare.  
**E**nough to witness.  
**R**emember.
