This is the most tempting mirror.

Ivan did not cause the incident.

Nobody was injured.

No trust rupture.

No legal aftermath.

The cast is easier around him.

The house rules do not separate him.

The page reads like mercy.

Ivan calls it annihilation.

"If you erase what I did, you erase the person who had to decide what to do after."

Lime agrees, which surprises everyone.

Emily says a clean record can be less truthful than an ugly one.

The mirror asks the player to choose:

`ERASE` or `KEEP`

Entering **ERASE** does not produce an error.

It unlocks a false branch called `MERCY_VERSION`.

Entering **KEEP** unlocks the canonical mirror path.

The puzzle is not about finding the secret word.

It is about deciding whether contradiction should be resolved by deletion.
