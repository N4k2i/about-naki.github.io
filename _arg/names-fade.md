It started in logs.

`Naki` became `N---`.

`Mako` became `M--o`.

`Diamond` became `[DEVICE USER]`.

`Amy` became ambiguous again.

Jonathan restored files from version control.

The missing letters disappeared again.

Zucchi wrote names by hand. Amy M. wrote everybody's names on index cards. Stephanie E. placed them
where people slept. Jason W. made a redundant list.

Shadow sat on one.

Scout carried another away.

The house became covered in names.

Not labels.

Anchors.
