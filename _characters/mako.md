## Overview

**Mako** is one of the central characters retained in the current *Found in the Noise* rewrite.

His clearest current-canon information is his family structure: he has an **adoptive younger brother, Markov**, and a **biological younger sister, Amy**.

## Family

### Markov

Markov is Mako's **adoptive younger brother**.

This distinction matters. Earlier continuity statements that Mako had no brother are superseded only in the adoptive sense; Markov is **not** Mako's biological brother.

### Amy

Amy is Mako's **biological younger sister**.

She lives with Mako and Markov.

## Household

The current continuity treats **Mako, Markov, and Amy** as part of the same household/family unit.

Amy's absence from an earlier house-tour event does not contradict this arrangement; she was away at the time.
