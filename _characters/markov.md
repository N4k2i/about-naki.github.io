## Overview

**Markov** is Mako's **adoptive younger brother** in the current *TAHAUFYA: Found in the Noise* continuity.

He is a **Noctaruk**, and Noctaruks are explicitly a separate species from sawians.

## Species

- **Species:** Noctaruk
- **Sawian:** no
- **Body type:** humanoid but strongly shark-like and nonhuman

## Established appearance

Markov has:

- **black-and-white fur**
- a **humanoid shark-like body**
- **digitigrade legs**
- a **shark tail**
- **fin structures**
- a **defined snout**

These are active design anchors, not optional embellishments.

## Silhouette rules

Markov should remain visibly nonhuman even at a distance. The digitigrade legs, tail, fins, fur, shark-like body structure, and defined snout should all contribute to that silhouette.

## Family

### Mako

Mako is Markov's **adoptive older brother**.

The relationship is adoptive rather than biological.

### Amy

Amy is Mako's biological younger sister and lives in the same household with Mako and Markov.

## Canon safeguards

Markov should not be rewritten as:

- a sawian
- Mako's biological brother
- a generic human
- a smooth-skinned shark humanoid with the established fur removed
- plantigrade if the current design is meant to retain digitigrade legs
