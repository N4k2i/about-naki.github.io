## Overview

**Makoe** is one of the five central siblings in the newest *Casualties: Unknown — Broken Lives* continuity.

The current core is:

1. **Outlete** — eldest
2. **Makoe**
3. **Tycone**
4. **Dote**
5. **Enigme**

Makoe, Tycone, Dote, and Enigme are the **same age**. Outlete is the eldest.

## Family

Makoe's current sibling group is Outlete, Tycone, Dote, and Enigme.

The siblings live within the same central family setup, and their murders remain part of the story's pre-Expie setup.

## LitRPG role

*Broken Lives* is now a progression-fantasy / LitRPG project. Makoe's profile is therefore designed to eventually track:

- level
- stats

- skills
- abilities
