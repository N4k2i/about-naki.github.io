## Goal

A very large advancement tree needs organization more than raw quantity.

## Recommended structure

Advancements can be grouped into:

- Onboarding
- Colors
- Materials
- Puzzle mechanics
- Exploration
- Collection
- Challenge
- Mastery
- Secrets
- Meta-completion

## Design rule

Avoid making every advancement a trivial counter.

A large system stays interesting when requirements vary: discovery, crafting, solving, mastery, exploration, unusual combinations, and long-term completion goals.
