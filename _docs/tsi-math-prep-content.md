## Content categories

The project can support:

- Math
- Reading

## Question record concept

A question record can include:

```yaml
id: algebra-linear-001
subject: math
topic: linear-equations
difficulty: 2
prompt: "Solve 3x + 5 = 20"
answer: "5"
explanation: "Subtract 5, then divide by 3."
```

## Mastery

Mastery should be tracked separately from whether a player has ever answered a question correctly.

A player who succeeds once may still need additional practice before a topic is considered mastered.
