A new YAML block appeared in generated HTML. It was not in `_data/profile.yml`.
It looked like the blocks Jonathan wrote for characters, except the subject was him.

```yaml
title: Jonathan Yaryan
alias: Nawkies
type: AUTHOR
world: REAL
control: FALSE
witnesses:
  - Stephanie E.
  - Jason W.
  - Amy M.
  - Shadow
  - Scout
```

The line that bothered him was not `world: REAL`.

It was `control: FALSE`.

Jonathan had spent years making fictional people by writing their names down and deciding what
followed those names. Even when characters surprised him, he understood the boundary: nothing
happened unless somebody wrote it, imagined it, drew it, coded it, or remembered it.

The page asserted the opposite.

It also asserted that his family were witnesses before any of them had witnessed anything.

At 03:02, a quiet impact came from the hallway.

Shadow ran.

Scout did not.

Jonathan stepped into the hall.

There was a person sitting against the wall.

He was wearing glasses.

One eye was amber-orange.

The other was darker red-brown.

He looked at Jonathan for several seconds and said, very softly:

"You look worse in person."

## 18+ ARCHIVE LAYER

The mature archive does not soften the panic of the first breach. Jonathan's first response is not wonder.
It is the sick certainty that something has crossed a boundary he does not understand and that his family is
now inside the consequences of a project he thought could never physically touch them.

The house does not feel haunted. It feels **compromised**.

Nobody knows whether the next person through the hallway will be frightened, dangerous, injured, unstable,
or carrying a history that was only ever safe because it existed behind a screen.
