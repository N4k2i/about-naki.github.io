The Witness Layer reveals the third hidden rule:

**Recognition stabilizes better than specification.**

A dossier can preserve facts.

A witness preserves the relationship between facts and a person.

Shadow and Scout matter because they recognize without reading.

Stephanie E., Jason W., and Amy M. matter because they met the cast as people before the archive
could teach them every field.

Jonathan matters differently.

He knew too much description before recognition.

That is why his arc requires learning to see without retrieving.
