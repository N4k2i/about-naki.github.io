A weaker ARG would treat the family as NPCs surrounding the author.

This ARG cannot.

Jonathan's real life is not a blank room containing only him.

Stephanie E., Jason W., Amy M., Shadow, and Scout are part of the environment that makes "Jonathan's
world" a world rather than an author avatar standing in empty space.

The bridge required witnesses who were not authored by Jonathan.

That is why the family matters mechanically.

They are not emotional decoration.

They prevent the ARG from becoming a closed loop where Jonathan writes evidence proving Jonathan's
own story.
