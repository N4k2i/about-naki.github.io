Cameras kept failing.

Zucchi's drawings did not.

She drew every arrival—not perfectly, which mattered. A camera tried to decide whether the cast
belonged in the image. Zucchi's hand did not care about database consistency.

Jonathan scanned the sketches.

The scans preserved them.

The archive added:

`STABILITY SOURCE: INTENTIONAL HUMAN MARK`

Zucchi crossed out **HUMAN** and wrote **PERSON**.

The field stayed corrected.
