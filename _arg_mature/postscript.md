Months later, the footer still reports:

`0.0001%`

Jonathan has tried removing it.

It returns.

The number does not increase when he writes.

It increases when somebody on the other side chooses to send something.

That distinction is the ending.

Stephanie E. no longer asks whether the site changed every time Jonathan opens it.

Jason W. still has the protocol.

Amy M. still has the failed photo.

Shadow and Scout are still siblings, still cats, and still uninterested in literary theory.

TAHAUFYA continues.

The real household continues.

On some nights the signal changes to:

`0.0002%`

When it does, Jonathan leaves the hallway light on.

Not because he expects anyone.

Because home can keep a light on without demanding that somebody return.
