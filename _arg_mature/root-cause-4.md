Shadow and Scout never read the bible.

They never debate authorship.

They never care whether Naki is protagonist-adjacent, whether Emily is opposition, whether Ivan
deserves trust, whether Markov's adoption has thematic weight, or whether a mirror record is canon.

They react to presence.

That makes them bad philosophers and excellent controls.

The ARG's deepest witness principle is intentionally mundane:

**If the cat keeps looking at the chair, check the chair.**
