Ivan voted stay.

The room reacted badly.

Some assumed it meant escaping consequences.

Ivan said the opposite.

"If I go back, the incident is what I am forever."

Lime reminded him accountability did not disappear outside.

Ivan said he knew.

Emily asked whether staying entitled him to trust.

He said no.

Jonathan could not decide whether the answer represented growth, self-interest, performance, or all
three.

That uncertainty is why the choice belongs to Ivan.

The ARG refuses to resolve him into a clean redemption.

## 18+ DECISION LAYER

The vote is crueler when every option can plausibly destroy somebody. Returning means surrendering part of the freedom
they fought to prove. Staying means risking erasure. The third answer is mostly theory and could fail catastrophically.

People argue. Some accuse others of cowardice. Some apologize later. Some do not.

Consent under impossible circumstances is still consent under pressure.
