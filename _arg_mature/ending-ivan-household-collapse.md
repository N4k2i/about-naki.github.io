## 18+ ENDING LAYER

The mature archive does not protect the reader from grief. Successful return still feels like bereavement.
Failure endings remain irreversible. Deaths stay dead. Relationships do not reset because a branch is labeled non-canon.

The point is not shock value.

The point is that choices matter only if the archive refuses to magically erase their cost.


### Mature Ivan Record

The 18+ archive does not minimize why the household feared Ivan. His past violence permanently changes how people
interpret his choices. When he becomes angry at being treated as dangerous, that anger does not magically make the
safety precautions unfair.

The mature route also refuses a simplistic answer: being feared does not make every future action inevitable.
When Ivan harms someone, the responsibility remains his.


### Mature Failure-Branch Rule

This branch contains irreversible death and severe grief. The archive does not offer resurrection, timeline reset,
or a secret 'actually they survived' reveal.
