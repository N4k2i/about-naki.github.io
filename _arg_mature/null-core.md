The NULL Layer reveals the fourth hidden rule:

**Every canon system requires something outside canon to compare versions.**

In the Character Bible, that thing is the writer.

In the household, it is independent witness.

In the ARG, it is the player.

The twenty-sixth slot was never a secret character.

It was the empty chair reserved for whoever was currently capable of saying:

"These two records disagree."

The horror is not that the slot is haunted.

The horror is that observation itself has a place in the architecture.

## 18+ NULL LAYER

The observer problem is allowed to become existential. If identity can occupy a structural slot, the player must confront
the possibility that some systems care more about **roles being filled** than about who fills them.

The archive does not claim that people are replaceable. It shows how easily systems can behave as though they are.
