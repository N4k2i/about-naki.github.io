One blanket remains folded differently from the others.

There is no supernatural reason.

Somebody from the cast preferred it that way during the weeks in the house.

Stephanie E. keeps folding it the same way after they leave.

Not as a shrine.

Not because changing it would break the bridge.

Simply because habit formed around another person and habit does not vanish when the person goes home.

Jonathan notices months later.

He almost asks whose blanket it was.

Then stops.

It matters that he cannot remember immediately.

It matters more that Stephanie still does.
