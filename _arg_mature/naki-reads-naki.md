The hardest line was not the heterochromia or the Expie classification.

**NEEDS: say what he needs before others have to infer it.**

Naki looked at Jonathan.

"Did I need that before you wrote it?"

Jonathan did not know.

"Did you write it because you saw it in me, or am I like that because you wrote it?"

Jonathan did not know.

"Do you understand how bad that answer is?"

Jonathan said yes.

Naki laughed once, without humor.

"At least that sounds like me."

Later he added a note under the dossier:

`I am allowed to disagree with a correct description.`

Jonathan never removed it.

## 18+ AUTHORSHIP LAYER

Reading the Character Bible becomes invasive in a way the clean archive only partially admits. The dossiers contain
psychological pressure points, relationship structures, failure modes, and notes about how each person is supposed
to behave under stress. Even compassionate guardrails can feel like somebody has been taking notes on your wounds.

The mature archive allows the characters to say that directly, including anger, profanity, disgust, and the accusation
that good intentions do not erase the violation of being readable to your creator.
