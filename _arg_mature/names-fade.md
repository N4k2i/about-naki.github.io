It started in logs.

`Naki` became `N---`.

`Mako` became `M--o`.

`Diamond` became `[DEVICE USER]`.

`Amy` became ambiguous again.

Jonathan restored files from version control.

The missing letters disappeared again.

Zucchi wrote names by hand. Amy M. wrote everybody's names on index cards. Stephanie E. placed them
where people slept. Jason W. made a redundant list.

Shadow sat on one.

Scout carried another away.

The house became covered in names.

Not labels.

Anchors.

## 18+ ERASURE LAYER

The erasure is more frightening in this archive. People lose seconds, then names, then the ability to trust their own
memory. Panic attacks, dissociation, rage, and helplessness become common. A character can be touching somebody's hand
while that person sincerely insists nobody is there.

The horror is not gore. It is being alive while the people who love you become neurologically unable to recognize you.
