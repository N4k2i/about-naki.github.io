Amy M. suggested the photo because the house needed proof that did not live inside Jonathan's code.

The first image showed Jonathan, Stephanie E., Jason W., Amy M., Shadow as a blur, Scout looking away—
and twenty-five distortions.

The second showed empty gaps.

The third showed pieces: Naki's glasses without a face, Markov's tail, part of Lime's wing, Deniy's
eyes, Diamond's device floating at chest height, Zucchi's sketchbook held by nobody.

The fourth looked normal.

That was worse.

It showed the household without the cast: the photograph reality expected.

Twenty-five people standing in the room could see the image erasing them.

Jonathan deleted it.

The phone restored it from backup.

`FAMILY_CANONICAL.jpg`

Nobody took another group photograph for weeks.

## 18+ HOUSEHOLD LAYER

The mature version treats the household emergency as exhausting rather than whimsical. Privacy collapses.
People sleep badly. Arguments happen because twenty-five traumatized strangers and six household members cannot
share limited space without friction. Fear turns ordinary noises into threats. Nobody has enough information,
money, room, or emotional capacity.

Stephanie E., Jason W., and Amy M. are forced to live with consequences they never consented to when Jonathan
created the story. Jonathan knows that. The guilt becomes harder to compartmentalize every day.
