The "noise" was never random corruption.

It was the information discarded whenever one system forced another into a simpler version.

When reality chose one breach memory, the other versions became noise.

When canon removed an old draft, structural remnants became noise.

When a camera erased an impossible body, the missing pixels became noise.

When a name degraded, the lost letters became noise.

When Jonathan reduced a person to dossier fields, everything the fields could not express became noise.

**Found in the Noise** was always about what survives outside the clean signal.

The ARG makes the title literal.
