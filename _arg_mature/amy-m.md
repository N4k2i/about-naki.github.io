Amy M. handled the name collision better than the archive did.

The site repeatedly rendered `AMY` without distinguishing the family member from Mako's biological
younger sister.

AMY ENTERED KITCHEN.  
AMY ASKED JONATHAN A QUESTION.  
AMY SAT BESIDE MARKOV.  
AMY FED SHADOW.  
AMY SAID SHE DID NOT WANT TO BE CALLED THE REAL ONE.

The final line belonged to Amy M.

"If she's fictional Amy, that makes her less real. If I'm real Amy, that makes her fake.
Just use our names correctly."

They wrote **Amy M.** and **Amy**.

It was the first time somebody in the house edited the archive to protect a character from the archive.

Jonathan saved the change.

Amy asked, "Did that become canon?"

Nobody laughed.
