Sododa, Polar, Redsky, Raine, Deniy, Grotti, Vesta, Blade, and the others eventually tried to be
funny again.

The first attempt was tiny.

Raine moved a harmless object. Redsky delivered the reveal with ridiculous ceremony. Polar apologized
to the object. Sododa laughed too early. Grotti called it the best and worst prank ever. Deniy tried
to escalate. Vesta stopped him. Blade watched Naki before joining.

Jonathan's family laughed.

That mattered more than the prank.

For one moment the cast were not evidence, refugees, anomalies, or moral questions.

They were annoying houseguests doing something stupid.

The happiness hurt afterward because everybody noticed how rare it had become.
