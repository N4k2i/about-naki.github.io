Mako insists there was a minute before Markov arrived when the hallway was full of seawater.

Nobody else remembers water.

No wet carpet existed afterward.

No camera captured it.

Markov, however, remembers the smell.

That is the problem.

Two witnesses share a memory with no physical trace.

The archive labels it:

`NON-CANON EVENT / SHARED MEMORY / DO NOT DELETE`

The page contains a six-line note:

**R**eality kept the dry hallway.  
**E**vidence kept the smell.  
**M**ako kept the water.  
**E**veryone else kept nothing.  
**M**arkov kept one detail.  
**B**oth versions still happened to somebody.

What word do the first letters spell?
