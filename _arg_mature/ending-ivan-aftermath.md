TAHAUFYA continues.

That is the cruel part.

The story does not end because Jonathan does.

Morning still exists.

Rooms still need opening.

People still eat.

People still argue.

Characters still have relationships, routines, unfinished conversations, and scenes that would once
have become chapters.

But six absences are carried back with them.

Naki remembers Jonathan.

Amy remembers Amy M.

Mako remembers Stephanie E. and Jason W.

Markov remembers Scout.

Scarf remembers Shadow.

The others remember all six in overlapping ways.

No single character carries the whole household.

Together they do.

The Character Bible gains a section nobody wrote:

## EXTERNAL WITNESSES — LOST

- Jonathan Yaryan / Nawkies
- Stephanie E.
- Jason W.
- Amy M.
- Shadow
- Scout

Underneath:

`DO NOT CONVERT TO CHARACTERS.`

`DO NOT RECREATE AS REPLACEMENTS.`

`MEMORY IS NOT OWNERSHIP.`

The cast agrees to keep the names.

Nothing more.

No invented dialogue.

No imagined final thoughts.

No reconstructed deaths.

No attempt to make them fit the story world after death.

They remain people from another world who were known, lost, and remembered.

For once, the archive understands that preservation can mean refusing to elaborate.
