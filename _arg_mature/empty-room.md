Twenty-five people left.

Nobody disappeared.

That was success.

Jonathan hated it.

Blankets were folded. Chairs returned. No wing brushed a doorway. No digitigrade footsteps crossed
the hall. No synthesizer spoke from another room. No prank-war group debated whether something stupid
was funny. No fictional Amy answered when Amy M.'s name was called.

Shadow walked to an empty sleeping place and sat.

Scout waited outside the room Naki and Blade had used.

Jonathan's family did not tell the cats there was nobody there.

They had learned better than to be certain.
