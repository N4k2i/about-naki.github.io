The obvious version of the ARG:

Characters escaped from fiction into reality.

The deeper evidence suggests something stranger.

The Character Bible remained stable.

The cast outside became unstable.

Reality kept trying to compress contradictions.

Recognition stabilized them.

The doorway stabilized only when neither side claimed ownership.

That pattern suggests the cast did not physically **leave** the story.

Instead, the household temporarily entered a shared continuity layer where story-logic and real-world
witness could overlap.

The distinction matters.

An escape implies one world lost people.

An overlap implies both worlds gained a boundary.
