Emily says a clean record can be less truthful than an ugly one.

The mirror asks the player to choose:

`ERASE` or `KEEP`

It unlocks a false branch called `MERCY_VERSION`.

Entering **KEEP** unlocks the canonical mirror path.

The puzzle is not about finding the secret word.

It is about deciding whether contradiction should be resolved by deletion.

## 18+ MIRROR LAYER

Mirror records become psychologically dangerous because a better version of history can look like mercy.
The mature archive allows characters to want the false version—even when accepting it would erase the person
who survived the real one.

Some alternatives are cleaner because they amputate the difficult parts.
