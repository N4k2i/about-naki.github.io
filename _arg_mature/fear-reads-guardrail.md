Fear found the line saying her autism was one part of her character, never the sole explanation
for her behavior and never a reason to infantilize her.

She agreed with it.

That did not make reading it easy.

"So you had to write down that people shouldn't do that?"

Jonathan explained that character bibles used guardrails against flattening characters.

Fear understood the writing logic.

"I hate that the writing logic is necessary."

Stephanie E. sat nearby without trying to turn the moment into a lesson.

Fear scrolled onward herself.

## 18+ AUTHORSHIP LAYER

Reading the Character Bible becomes invasive in a way the clean archive only partially admits. The dossiers contain
psychological pressure points, relationship structures, failure modes, and notes about how each person is supposed
to behave under stress. Even compassionate guardrails can feel like somebody has been taking notes on your wounds.

The mature archive allows the characters to say that directly, including anger, profanity, disgust, and the accusation
that good intentions do not erase the violation of being readable to your creator.
