The Character Bible behaves like a graph: people are nodes, relationships are edges, redlines are constraints, chronology gives direction, and household/friend groups form clusters. Losing one character weakens every edge attached to them. Continuity is relational, not merely individual.

## 18+ CHARACTER LATTICE NOTE

The relationship graph has consequences. When one node weakens, friends and family experience fear, grief, and sometimes
anger toward the person they are terrified of losing. Relationships stabilize continuity, but they also multiply the pain
of instability.
