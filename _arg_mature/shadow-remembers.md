At 01:18, everybody in the living room forgot Scarf.

Not his name.

Him.

The chair looked empty. Conversation had a missing rhythm nobody could identify.

Then Shadow walked across the room, jumped onto the chair, and curled against empty air.

Scout approached and sniffed the same place.

Jonathan took a photograph.

It showed two cats pressed against a distortion shaped like a person.

Memory returned.

Scarf was crying.

He kept petting Shadow because the cat had interacted with him during the gap.

The archive generated a phrase:

**SHADOW REMEMBERS.**

This is not metaphor.

Enter it exactly.

## 18+ ERASURE LAYER

The erasure is more frightening in this archive. People lose seconds, then names, then the ability to trust their own
memory. Panic attacks, dissociation, rage, and helplessness become common. A character can be touching somebody's hand
while that person sincerely insists nobody is there.

The horror is not gore. It is being alive while the people who love you become neurologically unable to recognize you.
