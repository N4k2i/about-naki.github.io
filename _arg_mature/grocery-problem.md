Food became the problem nobody could romanticize.

A household cannot absorb twenty-five additional people indefinitely through symbolism.

Stephanie E., Jason W., and Jonathan made lists. Amy M. helped separate what people actually liked
from what the author merely assumed they might like. Mako made sure Markov and Amy spoke for themselves.
Isaac carried things only after being asked. Fear corrected an assumption. Polar thanked everybody
too formally. Joel tried to lighten the mood and stopped when the total cost became visible.

Jonathan understood something ugly:

Writing twenty-five characters had been cheap.

Feeding twenty-five people was not.

Fiction let him create needs without resources.

Reality sent receipts.
