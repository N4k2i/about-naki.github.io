Could the cast be considered people if they had consciousness, memory, preferences, and bodies?

Could Jonathan be responsible for harm written before their real-world existence?

Was the Character Bible evidence, authorship, medical record, creative work, or something with no
category?

Diamond organized the questions.

"For someone outside this house to recognize them without owning them."

Nobody had a path to that goal.
