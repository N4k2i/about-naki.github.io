The ARG does not know your name.

It should not.

It does not need your location, identity, camera, microphone, or account.

It needs only one property:

**You can compare records without belonging to either continuity.**

That makes the reader a temporary external witness.

Not a character.

Not an author.

Not canon.

The NULL slot holds that structural role while the page is open.

Close the tab and the slot empties.

This is why the ARG never asks for real-world proof.

The puzzle exists entirely inside the archive.

## 18+ NULL LAYER

The observer problem is allowed to become existential. If identity can occupy a structural slot, the player must confront
the possibility that some systems care more about **roles being filled** than about who fills them.

The archive does not claim that people are replaceable. It shows how easily systems can behave as though they are.
