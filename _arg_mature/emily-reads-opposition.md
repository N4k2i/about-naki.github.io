Emily disliked **antagonist** more than any accusation.

She understood legal opposition. Procedure. Strategy. Framing.

Antagonist positioned her relative to a protagonist before she spoke.

Emily asked why protagonists received the benefit of that distinction more often.

Lime looked ready to answer.

"I know what I did in the story. I want to know whether I was ever allowed to be right about anything
that mattered."
