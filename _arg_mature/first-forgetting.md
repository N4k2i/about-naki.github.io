It lasted eleven seconds.

Jason W. entered the room, saw an occupied chair, and asked Jonathan why nobody was sitting there.

Markov was in the chair.

Everybody else could see him. Jason could see the chair deform beneath his weight, hear him speak,
see Shadow stare directly at him.

But for eleven seconds his mind treated every clue as a contradiction.

Then Markov returned to him all at once.

Jason sat down.

Markov stood.

Nobody knew which reaction was kinder.

The website recorded:

`REALITY CONSISTENCY FAILURE: 00:00:11`

`SUBJECT: MARKOV`

`WITNESSES MAINTAINING CONTINUITY: 30`

The count included the cats.

## 18+ ERASURE LAYER

The erasure is more frightening in this archive. People lose seconds, then names, then the ability to trust their own
memory. Panic attacks, dissociation, rage, and helplessness become common. A character can be touching somebody's hand
while that person sincerely insists nobody is there.

The horror is not gore. It is being alive while the people who love you become neurologically unable to recognize you.
