Jonathan writes that he misses them and deletes the sentence.

He writes that he is sorry and deletes that too.

Sorry is too broad. It asks the reader to supply the accusation.

He writes names instead.

Naki. Mako. James. Doc. Scarf. Ivan. Markov. Amy. Isaac. Fear. Zucchi. Blurb. Joel.
Blade. Lime. Aztec. Diamond. Sododa. Polar. Redsky. Raine. Deniy. Grotti. Vesta. Emily.

Twenty-five names.

Then six more:

Jonathan. Stephanie E. Jason W. Amy M. Shadow. Scout.

The ARG taught him that lists can be cages.

It also taught him lists can be rosters during an emergency.

Meaning depends on what the list is allowed to do.

He saves the file under `not-a-dossier.txt` and never links it anywhere.
