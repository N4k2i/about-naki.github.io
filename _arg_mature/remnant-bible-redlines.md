Every character-bible redline implies a failure mode.

Do not infantilize Fear.

Do not reduce Diamond's synthesizer to personality.

Do not reset Ivan's harm for comedy.

Do not flatten Mako's protectiveness into control.

Do not make a prank continue after genuine distress.

A redline is future-facing.

It is also archaeological.

It tells you what the writer is afraid a future version might do.

The remnant puzzle asks you to read the redlines not as rules, but as **fossil evidence** of possible bad timelines.

Take the first word after every "Do not" in the five lines above.

Their initials form a key.
