The file was **212 pages**.

Jonathan had built it to keep the cast consistent: Locked Canon, Bible Expansion, Open Field.
A system designed to prevent contradictions.

The characters read it as a record of being watched.

Naki found his wants and needs. Mako found descriptions of how he protected people. Markov found
adoption turned into a writing theme. Fear found a guardrail against infantilizing her. Diamond
found a reminder that their synthesizer was a tool, not a personality. Ivan found the incident
described as serious and consequential. Emily found herself labeled a competent legal antagonist.
Blade found the chronology of intimacy and relationship change. Joel found a warning about knowing
when the joke should stop. Deniy found a sentence about genuine distress.

Nobody could agree whether the document was compassionate.

It tried to protect them from bad writing.

It also proved that somebody had written instructions for how their pain should look.

## 18+ AUTHORSHIP LAYER

Reading the Character Bible becomes invasive in a way the clean archive only partially admits. The dossiers contain
psychological pressure points, relationship structures, failure modes, and notes about how each person is supposed
to behave under stress. Even compassionate guardrails can feel like somebody has been taking notes on your wounds.

The mature archive allows the characters to say that directly, including anger, profanity, disgust, and the accusation
that good intentions do not erase the violation of being readable to your creator.
