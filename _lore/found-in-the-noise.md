## Current family structure

- **Markov** is Mako's **adoptive younger brother**.
- **Amy** is Mako's **biological younger sister**.

## Markov / Noctaruk continuity

Markov is a **Noctaruk**, not a sawian. His established appearance includes black-and-white fur, a humanoid shark-like body, digitigrade legs, a shark tail, fin structures, and a defined snout.

## Canon policy

When an older draft conflicts with the rewrite, the rewrite wins. Older physical descriptions, relationships, and cast structures should not be restored unless they are explicitly reconfirmed.
