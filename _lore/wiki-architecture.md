## Record model

The site separates information by responsibility:

- **Characters** store identity and relationship information.
- **Species** store shared biological or visual rules.
- **Lore** stores world rules, systems, locations, and continuity concepts.
- **Docs** store technical project documentation.
- **Roadmaps** store future work.
- **Galleries** organize visual references.
- **Changelogs** preserve history.

## Why this matters

A wiki becomes hard to maintain when every page duplicates the same facts. Cross-linking allows one concept to have one canonical home.
