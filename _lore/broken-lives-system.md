## Overview

*Casualties: Unknown — Broken Lives* uses explicit LitRPG progression as a central story mechanic.

## Current five-sibling core

- **Outlete** — eldest; they/them
- **Makoe**
- **Tycone**
- **Dote**
- **Enigme**

Makoe, Tycone, Dote, and Enigme are the same age.

The siblings' murders remain part of the setup before the Expie progression portion of the story.

## System categories

- levels
- stats
- classes and roles
- skills
- abilities
- quests and objectives
- experience gain
- system notifications
- unlocks
- evolutions
- power growth
- territory progression

## Ashen Maw milestone

After the Fivefold Ascendancy gains the **Ashen Maw Outer Territory**, Makoe's four recurring best friends enter and are added to the system: Blurblee, Jamesz, Feare, and Zucchii.

## Antagonists

- **Ivane** — retained murderer from the story setup
- **Veeloo** — female recurring major antagonist
- **Leeroi** — male recurring major antagonist

Veeloo and Leeroi are a couple.

## Design principle

The system should influence story choices, responsibilities, threats, territory pressure, and strategy rather than merely displaying numbers.
